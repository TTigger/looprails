# Rubric: writing-quality

> A living benchmark for written deliverables (articles, docs, posts). The validator scores each
> dimension 1–5 against the anchors, applies weights → 0–100 composite. **Tune the weights to your
> taste.** Judge the rendered text as a reader would; prefer **pairwise** against the best prior draft.

## Scale
1–5 per dimension. Anchors describe 5 / 3 / 1; interpolate 2/4.

## Dimensions

### 1. Clarity — weight 30
The reader understands each point on the first pass.
- **5** — Every sentence earns its place; no ambiguity; jargon defined or avoided.
- **3** — Mostly clear; a few sentences need re-reading.
- **1** — Vague, padded, or contradictory; the reader has to guess the intent.

### 2. Structure & flow — weight 25
Ideas are ordered so each one prepares the next.
- **5** — Strong arc; sections in the right order; smooth transitions; scannable.
- **3** — Reasonable order; one or two abrupt jumps.
- **1** — Disorganized; repeats itself; no discernible progression.

### 3. Voice & engagement — weight 20
It reads like it was written on purpose, for this audience.
- **5** — Distinct, appropriate voice; concrete and specific; keeps attention.
- **3** — Serviceable but flat; generic phrasing.
- **1** — Lifeless or off-tone for the audience; filler and clichés.

### 4. Correctness & support — weight 25
Claims are accurate and backed.
- **5** — Claims are correct and supported; examples/evidence where it matters; no overreach.
- **3** — Mostly accurate; one unsupported claim or minor error.
- **1** — Factual errors or unsupported assertions presented as fact.

## Threshold (pass)
- Composite **≥ 80**, AND no single dimension **< 3**.

## Notes
- Weights must sum to 100.
- **Pairwise**: pass only if the current draft is at least as good on every dimension AND higher composite.
- Judge the text as delivered. Do not credit an outline or intent that is not in the prose.
